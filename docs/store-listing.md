## Description

Overskin is a screenshot and screen-recording tool for Windows that does the editing for you.

VIDEOS THAT ZOOM IN ON EVERY CLICK
Record your screen and Overskin follows the action. The recording glides in on each click, follows the pointer, and zooms back out, so a quick demo looks professionally edited without opening a video editor. The cursor moves smoothly with no lag, lands exactly on every click and gets a touch of motion blur. Add sound from your microphone or your PC, your webcam in a corner, and a backdrop behind the window. Trim, crop, blur and add text in the built-in video editor, then save an MP4.

EDIT THE TEXT INSIDE A SCREENSHOT
Spotted a typo after you captured it? Select the words in the picture and type over them. New letters come out in the screenshot's own font, size and colour, so nobody can tell. Lift a word, an icon or a picture off and drag it somewhere else; the gap fills in behind it.

PRIVATE DETAILS HIDDEN FOR YOU
Before you share, Overskin finds email addresses, card numbers, phone numbers, street addresses, IP addresses and keys, and blurs them in one click. Turn it on for every capture and you never send one by mistake. Everything is read on your PC with Windows' own text recognition: nothing is uploaded.

EVERYTHING ELSE YOU NEED
• Capture an area, a window, the full screen or a whole scrolling page, stitched for you.
• The screen freezes while you choose, so menus and tooltips stay put, with a magnifier for the exact pixel.
• A full editor: arrows, boxes, text, highlighter, blur, pixelate, smart erase, crop and Beautify for a clean background and shadow.
• Copy the text in any screenshot, and search your whole library by the words in it, offline.
• Record GIFs with captions, and convert between PNG, JPG, GIF and MP4.
• Step guides: click through a task and get a numbered how-to as a picture, web page, Markdown or PDF.
• Workflows: one hotkey for a whole routine, such as capture, hide private details, add a watermark and copy.
• Pins: float any picture or window on top of everything, fade it, make it click-through, or add a drawing grid to copy from.
• A library of every capture, with thumbnails, search and clean-up.
• Global hotkeys for everything, a tray menu, dark and light themes, editor colour themes, and any accent colour.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no sign-in, no telemetry, and no network connections at all. Your captures stay in your Pictures folder, on your PC.

Overskin is open source under the MIT license.
